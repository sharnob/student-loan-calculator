Frequently Asked Questions

Is this student loan calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is intended to be available as a free public web tool.

Do I need to create an account?

No account, student profile, sponsor profile or case record is required to use the calculator.

Does the calculator save my student information?

The public version is designed as a one-time calculator rather than a case-management system. Avoid entering unnecessary personal information.

Are the results an official loan approval?

No. Results are estimates for calculation purposes only. Actual loan eligibility, pricing, fees and approval depend on the relevant lender's terms and applicable rules.
