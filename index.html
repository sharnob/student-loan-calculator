Frequently Asked Questions

Is this calculator free?

Yes. It is intended as a free public online student loan calculator.

Can I calculate a loan against an FDR?

Yes. Select FDR and enter the FDR amount, tenure and applicable rates.

Can I calculate a loan against savings?

Yes. Select Savings account and enter the savings balance, tenure and applicable rates.

Does it save my personal information?

No student or sponsor profile is required. The calculation runs in your browser for one-time use.

Are the results official?

No. Results are estimates for planning purposes and should be checked against the lender's current terms.
