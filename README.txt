STUDENT LOAN CALCULATOR - PUBLIC WEBSITE
========================================

This package is prepared as a public, one-time student-loan calculator.

IMPORTANT:
1. Upload all files/folders in this ZIP to your web host.
2. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN.com in robots.txt and sitemap.xml with your real domain.
3. Open the website and test the calculator on desktop and mobile.
4. Submit the real website URL to Google Search Console after deployment.

The original calculator code/formulas are retained as much as possible. The
public-facing package adds SEO metadata, FAQ content, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.

Before publishing, verify that the calculator does not expose any private data
or credentials and that all calculations match your intended lending rules.
